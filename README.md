# Project 2:NHS workforce vs Demand(Dec 24 - Mar 26)
**Objective:**Is NHS workforce growth keeping up with mental health demand?
**Data Source:**                                                                                                                                                       NHS workforce Statistics,HCHS monthly(England)-Dec 202024 to Mar 2026                                                                                                 https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/nhs-workforce-statistics/march-2026  
**Method:**                                                                                                                                                      -  --Extracted FTE(Full-Time Equivalent) from monthly publications                                                                                                        -Transposed from wide to long formart in Excel(Dec-24 to Mar -26)                                                                                                     -Created time-series line chart
**Key Findings:**                                                                                                                                                      -Total FTE:1,368,034(Dec-24) to 1,379,670(Mar-26)                                                                                                                     -Net Change:+11,636 (+0.85% over 16 months)=effectively flat                                                                                                          -Peak:Nov 25(1,372,429) and Dec 25(1,377,448)-summer/winter attrition                                                                                                 -Context:Mental health new referrals alone were 500,517 in Mar-26(project 1)
**Insight:**                                                                                                                                                           While total NHS Workforce is stagnant(+0.85%),demand remains high(>500k MH referrals/month)suggesting sustained pressure on existing staff.
